Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Quoridor ("the app") is a free board game for Android and the web made by the Quoridor developers ("we"). This page explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • If you sign in with Google: the name, email address and profile photo link from your Google account. We never see your Google password.
  • If you play as a guest: a random guest ID and a generated name such as "Guest 4821". No email is needed.
  • Your game profile: your display name (you can change it), games played, wins and losses, wins against the computer and online wins.
  • Online games: the room code, which two players are in a room, the moves and walls played, and the result.
  • Presence: the time you were last seen using the app, so we can show how many players are online.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of requests) for security and to prevent abuse.
  • Website usage: Vercel Web Analytics collects anonymous page views, referrers, and browser and device information to help us understand visits. It does not use tracking cookies.

We do not use advertising or collect your contacts or files.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account and stats.
  • To run online games between players.
  • To show your display name and photo to your opponent and, if you have won online games, to other signed-in players on the leaderboard.
  • To show the number of players online and the total number of players.
  • To understand how the website is used.

Your email address is used only to sign you in. It is not shown to other players.

Who handles your data

We do not sell or share your data for marketing. The app relies on these services that process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in (servers in Tokyo, Japan).
  • Google provides "Sign in with Google" if you choose it.
  • Vercel hosts the website and provides anonymous website analytics.

How long we keep it and how to delete it

We keep your data while your account exists. Guest accounts that are no longer used may be removed. You can change your display name at any time in the app. To have your account and data deleted, email us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days. Signing out of a guest account makes that guest account unreachable, but its data stays until it is deleted.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
